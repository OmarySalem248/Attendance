Attendance in Australian Schools

How often students turn up to school, by state, sector, year level and gender, 2014–2025.

Where students attend most

Use the slider to move through the years. The Northern Territory has the lowest attendance rate of any state or territory in every year.

By sector

Government, Catholic and Independent

Attendance rates are higher in private schools than government schools. However, across all sectors, attendance has yet to fully recover from COVID-19.

By year level

Older students attend less

Higher year levels have lower attendance rates.

Below 90%

Students missing more than one day in ten

Each circle shows the share of students who attend fewer than 90% of school days. The Northern Territory has the largest share.

By gender

Female vs male attendance

There is a difference in attendance rates between genders, and its direction depends on the sector. In general, female students have higher attendance than male students in government schools, while male students have higher attendance in private schools.

By Indigenous status

The gap is widening, fastest in remote areas

Since 2016, attendance has fallen in every remoteness area for both groups, but far more steeply for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander students. In very remote areas, their attendance dropped 12.2 percentage points to 54%, compared with a 4.2-point fall for non-Indigenous students.

Indigenous students by state

The Northern Territory stands apart

Each circle is a state or territory, moved apart so none overlap. In the Northern Territory, nearly 4 in 10 students are Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander, and those students attend only 58% of school days. A large Indigenous population does not always mean low attendance: Tasmania has the second-highest share, yet its Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander students attend more often than in any other state.

Choose a remoteness area to compare like with like. In very remote areas, attendance falls to 46% in the Northern Territory and around 53% in South Australia and Western Australia.

By remoteness

Where attendance is highest within each state

Metropolitan areas have the highest attendance rates, with some states making exceptions in certain years. The Northern Territory and Tasmania seem to be the only territories and states where attendance is consistently higher in remote areas than in metropolitan areas.

Data: Australian Curriculum, Assessment and Reporting Authority (ACARA), student attendance data, 2014–2025. No data was collected in 2020.
